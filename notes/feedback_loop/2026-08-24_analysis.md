### Capability Requests

- A planner needs evidence the standing prose could not supply. The part brief now receives corpus claims and placed full-document findings before durable placement, with no standing prose or inherited heading order.
- A writer needs to know what each planned section is for. Every section now carries what it establishes, why it belongs there, its assigned evidence, and its handoff to the next section.
- A manuscript run needs writing material without treating it as factual authority. Source role and factual authority are now independent typed contracts.
- A book part needs a real length contract. The plan now carries a typed word interval and the rewrite can save only a candidate inside it.
- A one-click subsection run needs the same push-first path as a sweep. Both planning entry points use the subsection's durable tree placement to retrieve evidence.

### Reasoning Quality

The run followed its inputs coherently but the inputs made the wrong answer cheap. Its brief explicitly required the historical accident-before-attack opening even while naming newer agentic campaigns as the center of gravity. The plan inherited that order, research was then generated mostly from the old passage, and downstream reviewers spent heavily repairing a premise the pipeline had already committed to. This was a workflow failure at planning, not a failure to obey a prompt.

The cost amplified after drafting: four broad reviewers plus source-oriented resolution produced 95 findings, and the rewriter answered each through a separate tool call. The final grew from 1,533 to 2,135 words despite a 1,534-word ceiling because the budget existed only as advisory prose and format checks ran before the final transformation.
