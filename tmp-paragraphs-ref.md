### Paragraph Structure Template

**[Bold summary of main point].** [Explanation with evidence and examples]. [Connection to broader context or next concept].

Every paragraph must begin with a bolded sentence that summarizes the paragraph's core claim. The reader should theoretically be able to read just the bolded paragraph headers and move on to the next paragraph to quickly parse the content of the entire text.

Remember to make every word count. If a sentence or paragraph doesn't add new information or clarify an existing point, remove it. The best technical writing feels effortless to read because it respects the reader's time and intelligence.

Every paragraph must have a clear purpose that advances the overall argument
